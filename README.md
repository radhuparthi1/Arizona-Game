# Saguaro Leap

An Arizona-themed endless runner. You play a jumping saguaro cactus racing the desert rim — through the Sonoran, Sedona’s red rocks, the Grand Canyon, Monument Valley, and a dark Arizona night sky.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or from this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080).

### Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Jump | Space, W, Up arrow, click, or tap |
| Double-jump | Jump again in the air |
| Pause | P or Escape |
| Mute | Sound button |

### What to chase

- **Turquoise, copper, and gold** — score, with airborne streaks
- **Chili peppers** — a burst of heat speed
- **Turquoise amulets** — one free hit
- **Canyon leaps** — wide Grand Canyon gaps with a gem arc and echo

Avoid tumbleweeds, sandstone, rattlers, hawks, barrel cactus, and the drop itself. Dust devils will fling you skyward.

Biomes cycle as you ride farther. High score is saved in the browser.
